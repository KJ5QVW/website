About Me

Hi! I'm Liam Sanders, and my amateur radio callsign is KJ5QVW.

I am a high school student in College Station, TX, and I have been interested in amateur radio since I was 13 years old. I plan on going into law enforcement and am a devout Christian. I volunteer with RUF International as we seek to spread God's Word to students from other countries.

My main interests in amateur radio include 2m/70cm local repeater work, DMR, CW (learning), packet email, and homebrew antennas.
