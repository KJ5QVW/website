About Me

Hi! I'm Liam Sanders, and my ham radio callsign is KJ5QVW.

I am a high school student in College Station, TX, and I have been interested in amateur radio since I was 13 years old. I plan on 911 dispatching for a bit before making the move to law enforcement, which has been a lifelong career goal of mine. My hobbies outside of radio include patch collecting, airsoft, photography, reading, listening to all kinds of music, and volunteering in the sound booth at my church.

My main interests in amateur radio include 2m/70cm local repeater work, DMR, ARES, and packet email through methods like Mobilinkd.
