Speed and spacing

Character speed controls the length of dits and dahs. Farnsworth spacing keeps characters at the chosen speed while adding more space between characters and words. This is useful for learning to copy at a comfortable pace.

Timing basics

  • A dit lasts one time unit.
  • A dah lasts three time units.
  • Elements inside a character are separated by one unit.
  • Standard spacing between characters is three units; between words, seven.

At higher speeds, focus on the complete sound pattern instead of translating every element individually.
